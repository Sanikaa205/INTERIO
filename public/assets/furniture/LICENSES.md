# Furniture model licences

The six small GLBs directly in this folder are generated by `scripts/generate-furniture-assets.mjs` from original INTERIO geometry and need no attribution.

The models in `models/` are third-party. All are from **Poly Haven** (https://polyhaven.com), which publishes every asset under **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**: free for any use, no attribution required (credit is given here anyway). Licence terms: https://polyhaven.com/license

Prepared offline from the 1k glTF downloads: packed into a single GLB, textures resized to at most 512 px and re-encoded, materials renamed to the `main` / `frame` convention (see README.md). Meshes are unchanged.
